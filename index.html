  • Integrates SIDER (Drug–Side Effect) with STITCH (Drug–Drug & Drug–Gene/Protein) into a unified 3-relation graph.
  • Eliminates transductive leakage via per-fold adjacency lists, train-only reverse edges, and train-only true-tail masking.
  • Trained with BPR loss, AdamW, cosine annealing, and degree-stratified 5-fold cross-validation on NVIDIA Tesla T4.
Architecture Specs & Examination Board

Model & Training Hyperparameters

  • Architecture: 3-layer Multi-Relational KGAT (d = 256, 10 sampled neighbors/node, mean pooling across 0-hop to 3-hop embeddings) + MLP scorer (768 → 256 → 64 → 1 with LayerNorm).
  • Optimization: BPR pairwise loss, AdamW (lr = 1e-3, weight decay = 1e-4), Cosine Annealing (T_max = 60), gradient clip ≤ 1.0, seed = 42.
  • Best Fold (Fold 3): ROC-AUC 0.9987, F1 0.9911 (31,234 TP, 30,979 TN, 407 FP, 152 FN).
